1. Introduction

Beijing Hailong Tengda Labor Service Co., Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website hailongtengda.com (the "Site") or use our mobile applications (the "Apps") available on Google Play and App Store.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our Site and Apps, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Site or use our Apps.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out forms on our Site or Apps
  • Register for an account
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Contact us for support
  • Use our mobile applications

This information may include: name, email address, phone number, postal address, job title, company name, and any other information you choose to provide.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site or use our Apps, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device identifiers
  • Referring website or source
  • Pages viewed and time spent on pages
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Geographic location (country and city)
  • Language preferences

3. Mobile Application Specific Information

3.1 Permissions We Request

Our mobile applications may request the following permissions:

  • Camera access - for document scanning and photo uploads
  • Photo library access - for profile pictures and document uploads
  • Location services - for location-based services and attendance tracking
  • Push notifications - for important updates and reminders
  • Network access - for API communications
  • Device information - for analytics and security

3.2 Data Collected Through Mobile Apps

Our mobile apps collect and process the following additional data:

  • HR management data (employee records, schedules, attendance)
  • Workforce management information
  • Business analytics data
  • Real-time location data (with consent)
  • Device performance data
  • App usage statistics

4. Use of Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our Site and Apps
  • To deliver the services you request, including HR management and workforce solutions
  • To improve, personalize, and enhance your experience
  • To understand and analyze how you use our Site and Apps
  • To develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • To communicate with you for customer service, updates, and marketing purposes
  • To process transactions and send related information
  • To send you emails and push notifications
  • To find and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

Our apps may display the following types of advertisements:

  • Splash Ads (开屏广告) - Full-screen advertisements displayed when the app launches
  • Rewarded Video Ads (激励视频广告) - Video advertisements that users can watch to receive rewards within the app
  • Interstitial Ads (插屏广告) - Full-screen advertisements displayed during natural transitions in the app
  • Banner Ads - Rectangular advertisements displayed at the top or bottom of the screen
  • Native Ads - Advertisements that match the form and function of the app's content
  • Display Ads - Standard graphical advertisements

5.3 Data Sharing with Advertisers

Our advertising partners may collect and use the following types of data:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA, GAID)

  • Demographic information
  • Interest-based advertising data
  • Conversion and attribution data

These partners use this data to deliver personalized advertisements, measure ad effectiveness, and improve ad targeting. Please refer to each advertising partner's privacy policy for more details on how they handle your data.

5.4 Ad Targeting Options

You may control ad personalization through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > View Ad Targeting Information > Turn off Personalized Ads
  • Android: Google Settings > Ads > Turn off Ad Personalization

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Site and store certain information. Cookies are files with small amounts of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

6.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies - Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies - Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Functionality Cookies - Remember your preferences and settings
  • Advertising Cookies - Used to deliver relevant advertisements
  • Third-Party Cookies - Set by our advertising and analytics partners

7. Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Secure data centers and hosting
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures

8. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

To determine the appropriate retention period, we consider:

  • The amount, nature, and sensitivity of the personal information
  • The potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure
  • The purposes for which we process your personal information
  • Whether we can achieve those purposes through other means
  • Applicable legal requirements

9. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Union)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access - Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification - Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Erasure - Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing - Request limitation of processing of your personal data
  • Right to Data Portability - Request transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you
  • Right to Object - Object to our processing of your personal data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent - Withdraw consent at any time where we relied on consent
  • Right to Lodge Complaint - File a complaint with a supervisory authority

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at privacy@hailongtengda.com.

10. Your Rights Under CCPA (California, USA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know - Request disclosure of personal information collected, used, disclosed, or sold
  • Right to Delete - Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out - Opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination - Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights
  • Right to Correct - Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Limit Use - Limit use of sensitive personal information

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@hailongtengda.com or call 1-800-PRIVACY.

11. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Site and Apps are not intended for children under the following ages:

  • United States: Under 13 years of age (per COPPA)
  • European Union: Under 16 years of age (per GDPR)
  • United Kingdom: Under 13 years of age (per UK GDPR)
  • Canada: Under 13 years of age (per PIPEDA)
  • Australia: Under 13 years of age (per Privacy Act 1988)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under the applicable age without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information as soon as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@hailongtengda.com.
