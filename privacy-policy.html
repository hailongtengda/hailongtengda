5. International Data Protection Compliance

5.1 GDPR Compliance (European Union)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Lawful Basis: We process data based on legitimate interest, consent, or contractual necessity
  • Data Subject Rights: Users have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and object
  • Data Protection Officer: Contact us at support@hailongtengda.com for privacy concerns
  • Cross-Border Transfers: Data may be transferred outside the EEA with appropriate safeguards
  • Data Breach Notification: We will notify supervisory authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach

5.2 CCPA Compliance (California, USA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of personal information collected, used, or shared
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of personal information
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against users exercising their rights

5.3 PIPL Compliance (China)

For users in China, we comply with the Personal Information Protection Law:

  • Consent-based data collection and processing
  • Clear purpose specification for data usage
  • Data minimization principles
  • Security measures implementation

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child protection laws:

  • Age Restriction: Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age
  • No Deliberate Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Parental Controls: Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's data
  • Age Verification: Users must verify they are at least 13 years old to use our services
  • International Compliance: We comply with similar child protection laws in other jurisdictions (GDPR-K, etc.)
