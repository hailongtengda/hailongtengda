What We Offer

Complete Business Solutions

From human resources to mobile technology, we provide end-to-end solutions for businesses of all sizes.

Human Resources Services

Complete HR solutions excluding licensed employment agency activities and labor dispatch services. Our services include recruitment support, employee onboarding, performance management, compensation and benefits administration, and workforce planning.

Labor Services

Professional labor services excluding labor dispatch. We provide skilled workforce solutions for various industries including construction, manufacturing, hospitality, and retail, ensuring compliance with local regulations.

Management Consulting

Management consulting services to optimize your business operations. We offer strategic planning, organizational restructuring, process optimization, and performance improvement solutions.

Information Consulting

Information consulting services excluding licensed information consulting. We provide market intelligence, business intelligence reports, and data-driven insights to support informed decision-making.

Market Research

Market research services to understand market trends, customer preferences, and competitive landscapes. Our research methodologies comply with international standards and local regulations.

Business Training

Business training services excluding licensed vocational training and educational training. We offer soft skills development, leadership training, and professional development programs.

Housekeeping Services

Housekeeping and cleaning services for residential and commercial properties. Our trained staff provides thorough cleaning, organization, and maintenance services.

Landscape Services

Landscape engineering services for residential complexes, commercial properties, and public spaces. From design to maintenance, we create and maintain beautiful outdoor environments.

Material Handling

Material handling and building materials sales for construction and logistics operations. We provide efficient material handling, warehouse management, and construction material supply.
